# Note Feed Support

This is where to report bugs, ask questions and suggest features for **Note Feed**, the iPhone and iPad app that keeps your notes, links, photos and files in one feed that reads like a chat with yourself.

The app's source code isn't here. This repository is only for support.

## Get help

[Open an issue](../../issues/new) and say which kind it is:

- **Bug:** something doesn't work the way it should
- **Question:** you're not sure how something works
- **Feature request:** something you'd like the app to do

Before opening a new issue, [search the existing ones](../../issues?q=is%3Aissue) in case someone has already asked. If you find a match, add a 👍 reaction or a comment instead of opening a duplicate. That helps show what matters most.

## Reporting a bug

A good bug report makes it much faster to fix. Please include:

1. **What happened**, and what you expected to happen instead
2. **Steps to reproduce it**, starting from opening the app
3. **App version:** in Note Feed, open Settings and look at the About row, for example "Version 1.0 (3)"
4. **Device and iOS version:** for example "iPhone 17, iOS 26.1" or "iPad Air, iPadOS 26.0"
5. **Screenshots or a screen recording**, if they help show the problem

### Keep your notes private

Issues here are **public**, and anyone can read them. Don't paste the contents of your real notes, and check screenshots before you post them. If a bug only happens with a certain note, try to reproduce it with a made-up note first, or describe it without the private details (for example "a note with a PDF and two photos").

## Common questions

**Where are my notes stored?**
Only on your device. Note Feed has no account and no server, and it doesn't collect or share your notes. When you add a link, the app loads that web page once to build the preview, the same way Safari would.

**How do I move my notes to a new iPhone?**
Your notes are included in your regular iPhone backup. Restore your new iPhone from an iCloud or computer backup, or use Quick Start, and your notes, photos and files come across with everything else.

**Note Feed isn't in my share sheet.**
In the share sheet, scroll to the end of the row of app icons, tap **More**, then **Edit**, and turn on Note Feed. You can drag it higher in the list to keep it handy.

**I tipped before. How do I get my supporter badge back?**
It comes back on its own. Open Note Feed while signed in with the same Apple Account you tipped with, and the app finds your past tip and restores the badge and app icon.

**How do I delete everything?**
Open Settings and tap **Delete all notes**. This permanently removes every note, including its photos and files, and can't be undone. Deleting the app also removes all of its notes.

## Feature requests

Ideas are welcome. Tell us what you're trying to do and where the app gets in the way, not just the feature you have in mind. Knowing the problem often leads to a better fix than the first idea.
