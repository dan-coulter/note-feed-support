# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** September 27, 2026

## Overview

Note Feed ("the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This privacy policy explains our privacy practices.

## Data Collection

The App **does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data or usage information**. 

- No account information is collected
- No tracking or analytics data is gathered
- No user behavior data is recorded
- No data is sent to external servers

## In-App Purchases

The App offers optional in-app purchases ("dev tips") through Apple's App Store. These transactions are processed entirely by Apple and are subject to Apple's privacy policies. We do not receive or store any personal information related to your purchases beyond what is necessary for providing the purchased features.

## Local Operation

The App operates entirely on your device. All functionality is performed locally without sending information to remote servers or third parties.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected with an updated "Last Updated" date above.

## Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact us through the project repository.
